Lokad.Svg contains managed adaptations of Skia analytic curve/line clipping,
polynomial solvers, vertical edge combination, horizontal clip paint routing,
whole-path mask/run dispatch bounds,
axis-aligned path mapping, raw control-point convexity classification and
antialiased rectangle paint-clip dispatch and byte coverage, hard mask-region rounding, rounded plain
rectangle coverage and ordered rectangle-frame hull/interior coverage,
shared filled/stroked-path rectangle recognition, native closed-rectangle stroke
contours and float fringe coverage, and line-segment
dash endpoint interpolation, isolated image-layer source-over, high-precision shader pixel storage,
constant shader construction and low-precision pixel replay, bilinear repeat
image-shader sampling, and
linear/radial gradient coordinate construction and straight open-stroke
normalization and join construction, clipped image-shader layer restoration,
rational round caps/joins, SVG endpoint-arc construction and conic subdivision,
live convex curve advancement, curve measurement and dash cutting, tight source-curve paint bounds,
rounded-rectangle conic source construction, bitmap-local transformed convexity classification,
inside focal-cone coordinate construction, nonzero clip bounds, per-bitmap clip border coverage
and clip construction order, and retained convex shape classification,
and antialiased line/curve hairline coverage/dispatch.
These adaptations do not depend on native Skia.
