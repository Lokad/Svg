# Glyph conic sampling centers

The bounded Windows conic equation retains a strict zero test at a sampling-cell
center after both axes are normalized. Straight borders retain their independent
directed ownership rule. The font regressions continue to protect layout, fitting,
positioning and preblend; references retain their captured bytes.

The [mechanism cases](native-centers.json) isolate a near-linear quadratic, its
chord, nearby controls, translations, reflections, reversal, exchanged axes and
scale. Twenty-one individually selected tests compare an explicit 8×8 native
alpha window through direct and prepared row scans. The em-32 instruction-free
face selects simple dropout control. Three cases fail before the fix; the other
eighteen protect nearby behavior. The complete native sheet remains available for
optional investigation; assertions are not batched to conceal test counts.

Two [canonical rendering cases](capture.json) retain the original supplied-Noto
context with black and blue paint. Black protects the coverage used by clip-mask
recording; blue protects color-dependent preblend. Both use one conversion path,
unchanged platform references and three fresh healthy browser captures per host.
Linux was already exact. Each Windows case previously differed at two pixels.

The numerical font recomposes one fitted Noto conic into a closed band; it is
distributed under the [OFL](OFL.txt), with a new family name. Noto itself is reused
from [supplied-font-labels](../supplied-font-labels/fonts/OFL.txt). The authored
SVG controls are CC0-1.0. The [font producer](../../../tools/build-conic-center-font.py)
rebuilds the committed numerical font byte-for-byte with FontTools 4.66.1.
No hosted SVG bodies are retained and ordinary tests require no browser or Python.

These sampling mechanisms also support [direct text clipping](../text-clip/README.md).
The pinned browser paints text
clips through a black mask; that coverage differs from ordinary colored glyph
paint. The text-clip collection qualifies font/resource selection and ordered clip
replay separately from these glyph controls. The browser's
[clip strategy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chromium/chromium/153.0.8010.52/third_party/blink/renderer/core/layout/svg/layout_svg_resource_clipper.cc)
and independently captured controls support that distinction.

[Qualification](qualification.json) records source identities, failing-before
checks, validation times and remaining acceptance gates.
